# IQRA School Website — Owner Guide

## Logging In

1. Go to **https://your-site-name.netlify.app/admin**
2. Enter your username and password
3. Click **Log In**

*(If this is your very first login, ask your developer for the starting username/password, then immediately go to **Account Settings** and change them — see below.)*

---

## Uploading Photos & Videos

1. Click **🖼️ Gallery Media** in the sidebar
2. Find the category you want (e.g. "Sports Day"), or create a new one first:
   - Under **Add New Category**, type a title (e.g. "Science Day 2027"), pick an emoji, choose a group (Events / Academic / Campus Life), then click **+ Add Category**
3. On the category card, click **+ Add Photos** or **+ Add Videos** and select your files
4. Wait for the upload to finish (large videos take a little longer) — it appears on the site automatically, no extra "publish" step needed
5. To remove a photo/video, click the **✕** on its thumbnail

**Tips:**
- Photos: JPG or PNG work best
- Videos: MP4 works best
- Very large videos take longer to upload — a stable internet connection helps

---

## Editing Admission Details

1. Click **📋 Admissions** in the sidebar
2. Edit the **Intro text** (the sentence shown at the top of the Admissions section)
3. Edit any step's title/description, or click **+ Add Step** for a new one, or **Remove** to delete one
4. Click **Save Admissions** — changes appear on the website immediately

---

## Managing Social Links

1. Click **🔗 Social Links** in the sidebar
2. To add one: enter a label (e.g. "YouTube"), the link URL, and pick an icon from the list — or choose **Custom Icon** to upload your own icon image
3. Click **+ Add Link**
4. To remove a link, click **Delete** next to it

These links appear as icons on the homepage and the floating WhatsApp button uses
whichever link is set to the WhatsApp icon.

---

## Changing Your Username & Password

1. Click **🔐 Account Settings** in the sidebar
2. Enter your **current password** (always required, for security)
3. Enter a new username and/or new password (leave either blank to keep it the same)
4. Click **Save Changes**

**Please do this immediately after your first login**, and choose a password
that's at least 8 characters and not easy to guess.

---

## Logging Out

Click **Log Out** at the bottom of the sidebar when you're done, especially on a
shared or public computer.
