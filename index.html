What We Offer

Our Programs

Two pillars of education — Deen and Dunya — offered together under one roof, ensuring a holistic upbringing for every student.

🕌 Islamic Education

Hifz ul Quran

A structured Hifz program for students who wish to memorize the Holy Quran under the guidance of qualified Huffaz with Sanad.

  • Personalized memorization plan
  • Daily revision sessions
  • Tajweed integrated from day one
  • Progress tracked by parents
📖 Islamic Education

Tajweed ul Quran

Master the rules of Tajweed to recite the Holy Quran with correct pronunciation, rhythm, and beautification as revealed.

  • Makharij & Sifaat taught in detail
  • Audio-visual teaching aids
  • Certification upon completion
  • Suitable for all ages
✨ Islamic Education

Nazra e Quran

Learn to read the Quran from scratch with Qaida, progressing to fluent Nazra reading. Perfect for beginners and young learners.

  • Noorani Qaida for beginners
  • Individual attention guaranteed
  • Short Surahs memorization
  • Islamic manners & duas included
🔬 Science & General

Science Education

Modern curriculum aligned with national standards, taught through interactive methods to spark curiosity and critical thinking.

  • Mathematics & English
  • General Science & Urdu
  • Social Studies & Islamiat
  • Computer basics (Grade 3+)
🎨 Early Years

Early Childhood

Playgroup and Nursery programs built on play-based learning, developing language, motor skills, and Islamic values from the very start.

  • Montessori-inspired activities
  • Arabic alphabet introduction
  • Duas & basic Kalimas
  • Art, craft & storytelling
🏆 Co-curricular

Extra Activities

Beyond the classroom — a range of enriching activities that build character, teamwork, creativity, and leadership in young Muslims.

  • Naat & Qiraat competitions
  • Science exhibitions
  • Sports & physical education
  • Annual Quran Khwani
